## Intent

Create a ruthless hackathon demo that markets Interleave by proving one concrete failure end to end. Open on the risk of a successful human edit being erased by an agent operation already in flight. Use the real Plane integration to show the native WebMCP dispatch, manual human edit, delayed overwrite, exact violated rule, minimal replay, guarded result, regression export, and tested upstream Plane patch.

The tone is controlled, technical, and confident. Every claim should be supported by a visible product receipt. The film should feel like evidence being assembled, not a generic startup trailer.
