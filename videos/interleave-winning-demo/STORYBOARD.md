## Video direction

- **Palette and type:** Use the captured Interleave inversion from `frame.md`: deep `cream` ground, pale `ink` voice, violet `coral` as the single voltage moment in each frame, and `tile`/`navy` only for proof surfaces. Use display/body/mono roles from the pack; mono carries commits, tool counts, event kinds, values, and receipts.
- **Motion grammar:** One continuous, restrained technical film. Every reveal follows the narration cue and settles on a smooth long-tail move. Use one motivated camera move per shot at most; element-level reveals carry the back half. Holds are still, with subtle jitter only when a focal would otherwise feel dead.
- **Rhythm:** Frames 1, 2, 4, 6, and 7 develop through the spoken line. Frames 3 and 5 deliberately hold long enough for judges to read the proof. Frame 8 resolves early enough to give the category claim and public route a clean final hold.
- **Composition:** Keep one dominant focal at three-to-one hierarchy, at least three depth layers for UI footage, and all load-bearing content above the bottom 17% caption band. Crop product footage to the evidence being discussed; avoid browser chrome and incidental navigation.
- **Never:** No front-loaded slideshow entrances, independent floating screensaver motion, lazy breathing, back-half camera drift, bounce, fabricated UI, generic AI gradients, heavy glow, fake cursors, or decorative geometry standing in for evidence.
